# Published reel covers

Source: https://www.facebook.com/kp468/reels/

Six original public JPEG covers acquired through the browser page asset export. Each filename is the exact Facebook reel ID, mapped in src/reels.js. Images are hosted locally to avoid expiring CDN links. No login screenshot, credentials, view counts or invented video titles are included. Cards open the matching reel in a new tab; they do not pretend to play video inline. Homepage and static portfolio share the same data.
