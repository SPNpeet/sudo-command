# Visual refinement — 3 October 2026

The previous client strip used a second utility header, small logos inside tall cards and repeated frames. These competed with the original client identities. Service recommendation pictures were too small beside the copy.

## Direction

- Preserve the original Sudo mark and orange, white and charcoal identity.
- Make actual work and original client logos the primary visual evidence.
- Use one outer client frame, larger logos, fine separators and a contrasting orange service ribbon. Keep seven evidenced brands; do not invent customer relationships.
- Retain continuous automatic motion with pause/resume and reduced-motion layouts. Client identities have no outbound links.
- Put recommendation photography above the story at full card width. Tighten typography, tags and spacing.
- Simplify hero framing, portfolio headings and photo proportions in the delivery, process and contact sections.
